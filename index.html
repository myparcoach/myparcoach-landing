The Problem

"Golfing potential depends primarily on a player's attitude and on how well he thinks."

— Bob Rotella, Sports Psychologist

If attitude and thinking drive potential, then the scorecard you play to shapes everything. A target you can't reach doesn't challenge your thinking — it ends it. By the fourth hole, the math has beaten you. By the turn, you've stopped competing.

MyParCoach puts a realistic number in front of you on every hole so your attitude has something to fight for — and your thinking has somewhere to go. That's not just a better scorecard. That's how potential actually moves.

Playing to the printed scorecard isn't a game plan.
It's a wish.

The par on that little white card was written for scratch golfers. Not you. Not most people who play this game. And yet every recreational golfer in the world tees it up on Saturday morning quietly hoping — this is the day — as if the golf gods are finally going to show up and hand them a 72.

Spoiler: they're not coming. That's not a game plan. That's a lottery ticket with a dress code.

Real game management means playing to a realistic target on every single hole — one that's calculated from your actual handicap, distributed across 18 holes using stroke index, just like a proper handicap system does. That's what MyParCoach gives you. Not hope. A number.

Game Management

What actually changes when you
play to your par.

🧠

You stay in the round

Making a 6 on a par 4 when your par is 5 is a bogey. Not a disaster. Not a reason to stop trying. You're one over through one hole. The round is alive — and your brain knows it.

🎯

You know your target on every tee

On your hardest holes, you get an extra stroke. On your easiest, you don't. That's exactly how a real handicap system works — and now you can see it, hole by hole, before you swing.

📈

You can actually measure improvement

When you track rounds against your MyPar over time, patterns emerge. You're leaking strokes on the par 3s. You're outperforming your target on holes 12–15. That's data. That's coaching material. That's how games improve.

🏌️

You play your game — not theirs

The touring pro's par is irrelevant to your round. Stop letting it set the terms of whether you succeed or fail. MyParCoach puts you in charge of the only scorecard that matters: yours.

How It Works

From the first tee to the 19th hole.
In four steps.

01
🔍

Find Your Course

Search from 10,000+ courses worldwide. Select your tee. The rating, slope, and hole-by-hole yardages load automatically.

02
🎯

Set Your Target

Enter your handicap index or a target score. MyParCoach distributes your MyPar across all 18 holes using stroke index — just like a real handicap system.

03

Manage Your Round

Your scorecard shows Hole · Par · Yds · SI · MyPar · Score · vs MyPar. See exactly where you stand against your targets after every hole — not some scratch golfer's dream score. Stay focused. Stay in it. Play the round you're actually capable of.

04
🤝

Play with Friends

Add up to four players, each with their own handicap. One scorecard tracks everyone's MyPar simultaneously — no math, no markup, no debate about who's winning. Every player sees their own targets, and the group sees the real standings after every hole.
