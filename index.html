The Problem

Most golfers lose focus by the 4th hole.

Course par is set for scratch golfers. When a 16-handicapper walks off the 3rd green having made a 6 on a par 4, they feel like they've already failed. That feeling compounds. By the turn, they've stopped trying.

It doesn't have to work that way. A 16-handicapper playing to their real targets — a mix of 5s and 4s on the par 4s based on stroke index — can walk off that same 3rd green having matched their par. The round stays alive. The focus stays sharp.

"The course par means nothing to a recreational golfer. What matters is whether they're playing their game well."

For Golf Coaches

Free for coaches.
Powerful for your students.
