Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

MyParCoach is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. We keep this simple because we are a small, independent app — not an advertising company.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address (used for login and password reset only)
  • Your password (stored securely by Supabase — we never see it in plain text)

When you use the app, we store:

  • Golf rounds you save — course name, date, scores, and handicap-related data you enter
  • Your handicap differential history (Pro users)
  • Whether your account is on the Free or Pro plan
  • A count of how many rounds and coach reports you have saved

We do not collect your name, location, phone number, payment card details, or any information beyond what is listed above.

What we do not collect

  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever
  • We do not share your data with third parties except as described below
  • We do not collect payment information — all payments are handled directly by Stripe

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the MyParCoach service:

  • Your email is used to log you in and send password reset emails
  • Your saved rounds and handicap data are used to display your history and calculate your handicap index
  • We may use your email to send you important service-related updates (not marketing)

Where your data is stored

Your account and round data is stored securely by Supabase, a cloud database provider based in the United States. Supabase is SOC 2 compliant and stores data with encryption at rest and in transit. You can read Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. MyParCoach never sees or stores your credit card details. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

Cookies

MyParCoach uses only essential session cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or any third-party tracking.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your round data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@myparcoach.com and we will respond within 5 business days.

Account deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, email support@myparcoach.com with the subject line "Delete my account." We will permanently delete your profile, rounds, and all stored data within 7 days.

Children's privacy

MyParCoach is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of MyParCoach after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@myparcoach.com — we are a small team and we read every message.
